## Slide 3: Technical Solution Architecture
### Multi-Layer Approach
- **Data Engineering:** SMOTE synthetic sample generation
- **Neural Architecture:** 3-layer Deep LSTM (128→64→32 units)
- **Stability Techniques:**
  - Batch Normalization
  - Variable Dropout rates
- **Mathematical Innovation:** Weighted Loss Function (1000x weight for rare cases)
